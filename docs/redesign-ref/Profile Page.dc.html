About

Amara Okonkwo has spent eighteen years rebuilding how mathematics is taught in under-resourced Nigerian classrooms. Her numeracy framework — designed for multi-grade rooms with one teacher — has been adopted by twelve schools across Enugu State and trains forty new educators each year.

She believes recognition should follow impact, not noise. This profile is part of the permanent continental record.
