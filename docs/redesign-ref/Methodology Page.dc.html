How the CPI is scored

The Cultural Power Index is a transparent, audited 0–1000 score. Every point traces back to a verifiable source — no editorial thumb on the scale.

The four factors

= 1000 max
Verified votes
400 pts

One OTP-verified vote per person per category. Bots and duplicates are removed before counting.

Documented impact
300 pts

Evidence of real-world contribution — programmes, reach, outcomes — reviewed by the panel.

Peer endorsements
200 pts

Weighted endorsements from already-verified peers in the same programme.

Reach & consistency
100 pts

Sustained engagement across the cycle, not one-off spikes.
