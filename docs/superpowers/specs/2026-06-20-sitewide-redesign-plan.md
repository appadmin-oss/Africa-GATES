### ⚠ CRITICAL accuracy notes for the remaining pages
- **Methodology / CPI formula — DO NOT use the design's numbers.** `Methodology Page.dc.html` shows a placeholder formula (Verified votes 40% / Documented impact 30% / Peer endorsements 20% / Reach 10%). The **REAL** model (per `integrity.twig` + `src/Services/NomineeScoringService.php` + `CpiService`) is:
  - **CPI = 45% public votes + 55% judge panel** (config-driven via `RuleEngine::weights()`, these are the defaults). Rationale on-page: popularity alone shouldn't win; the tilt to judges keeps merit over marketing.
  - **Judge panel = average of 4 criteria, 25% each: Impact · Originality · Reach · Integrity** (0–10 per criterion; complete scorecards only; quorum = `min_judges_per_nominee`, default ≥2).
  - **Community score normalises ORGANIC votes only** — donation/bonus votes count for display but are excluded from CPI (`organic_vote_count`).
  - When reskinning, apply the design's *visual treatment* (factor cards w/ bars, principles, timeline) but with these REAL factors/weights, and **preserve** integrity.twig's detailed sections (fraud scoring 0–100 bands, donation votes, audit trail, privacy) — the design is condensed and would drop them.
