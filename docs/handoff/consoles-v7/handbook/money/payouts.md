# Payouts and holds

Partners and referrers are paid on Fridays. A changed bank account holds the payout for a check.

> **Rule:** A payout is held when bank details changed in the last 7 days, until someone confirms the change with the account owner by phone.

- Payout runs Friday. Anything ready by Thursday 18:00 goes in it.
- Each partner sees what’s coming in their own area.
