# Refunds and disputes

Decide refunds within two working days. Answer card disputes before the bank’s deadline.

| Case | Default |
|---|---|
| Ticket, can’t attend, more than 48 h before | Refund |
| Ticket, within 48 h | Offer a transfer to someone else |
| Duplicate payment within minutes | Refund the duplicate |
| Vote contribution | No refund once votes are counted, except duplicates |

> **Rule:** Refunds up to ₦20,000 need one person; above that, a second person confirms.

> **Tip:** For a dispute, attach the receipt, the scan or vote record, and the terms version accepted.
