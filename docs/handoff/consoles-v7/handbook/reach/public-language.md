# What we never say in public

A short list of words and claims that never appear on a public page, email or letter.

> **Rule:** Never mention AI in public. Gee is just the name of our helper.

> **Rule:** Never describe voting in shopping language: no buy, cart, checkout or price.

> **Rule:** Never show the same fact twice on one screen.

- Say “nominee”, not “candidate”.
- Say “contribution”, not “payment”, for votes.
- Say what happens next, not “success”.
