# Moderation

Comments, messages and quotes stay hidden until a person looks. Reports from several people go first.

| Contains | Do |
|---|---|
| A phone number or address | Hide the detail, publish the rest |
| Abuse or a threat | Remove, and report the account |
| Campaigning for votes with money | Remove and flag the nominee’s page |
