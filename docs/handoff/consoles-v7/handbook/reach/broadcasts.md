# Broadcasts and quiet hours

Only opted-in people, never between 21:00 and 08:00, and the SMS cost is shown before you send.

1. **Pick the audience.** A segment of opted-in people. The count updates as you go.
2. **Write it.** Short, one action, one link.
3. **Check the cost.** Email is free. SMS shows its cost; a big SMS send is a request to comms.
4. **Schedule.** Quiet hours (21:00 to 08:00) are skipped automatically.

> **Rule:** Nobody is messaged without opting in to that channel.

> **Rule:** SMS spend over ₦50,000 a day pauses sends and tells finance.
