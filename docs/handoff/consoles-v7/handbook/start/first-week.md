# Your first week

What to do on days one to five so you know where things are before something goes wrong.

1. **Set up a passkey.** Settings › Security and sign-in. Console roles sign in with a passkey.
2. **Read How the console is laid out.** Four minutes. Then press ⌘K and find three pages by typing.
3. **Open one edition and one event.** Walk their menus. Look at Overview and Today: they tell you what needs a person.
4. **Read the playbook for your area.** Moderators: Reviewing nominations. Event leads: Planning an event. Finance: Reconciling payments.
5. **Shadow one decision.** Sit with someone while they approve a refund or a nomination, and read the audit entry it leaves.

> **Tip:** Home is sorted by consequence, not by date. Start every day at the top of it.
