# Your role and what it reaches

Access is given per page and, for event and award staff, per event or award. Every scoped access has an end date.

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Superadmin | Everything, including configuration, keys and roles. |
| Admin | Everything except configuration. |
| Editor | Content and programmes. Can draft a shortlist, can’t publish it. |
| Moderator | Moderation and nomination review, limited to the awards they’re given. |
| Event lead | One or more events, until the day after each one. Spends up to their limit; above it they ask. |
| Event team | The pages of one event their lead gives them, until the day after it. |

> **Rule:** The menu never offers a page your role can’t open. If you can see it, you can use it.

> **Rule:** Volunteers and judges never sign in to this console. They have their own areas.

If you need a page you can’t see, ask the person who gave you access. Event leads use Requests › Access for my team, which goes to an admin with a reason and an end date.

> **Watch out:** Never share a sign-in. Everything you do is logged under your name, including notes you add when you act.
