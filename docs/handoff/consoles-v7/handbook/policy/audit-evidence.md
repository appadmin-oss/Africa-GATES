# The audit log and evidence

Every change is logged with who, when and why. Entries and reviewed evidence can’t be edited.

> **Rule:** Evidence is immutable once reviewed. There is no edit or delete for it.

> **Rule:** Audit entries can’t be edited or deleted. A correction is a new entry.

> **Tip:** Add a note when you act. It’s the only place the why is kept.
