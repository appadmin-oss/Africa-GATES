# Setting up an edition

Nine steps from a blank edition to nominations open. Changes after opening publish as a new version.

1. **Basics.** Name, edition number, scope (state or LGA), and who runs it.
2. **Dates.** Six milestones: nominations open and close, shortlist, voting window, judging close, ceremony.
3. **Look.** Pick an identity family and a cover. Looks are presets; there are no custom colours.
4. **Categories.** Name, who it’s for, and the minimum nominations before it can be shortlisted.
5. **Nomination form.** System fields are locked. Add evidence and region fields as needed.
6. **Rules.** Categories per nomination (1 to 3), reason length, self-nomination, daily limit per person.
7. **Voting mode.** Free only, or free plus contribution tiers with a cap per person per category.
8. **Judging.** Panels, rubric criteria and weights. Weights lock when nominations open.
9. **Terms.** Eligibility, prize and terms. Nominators accept the version that was live when they submitted.

> **Watch out:** Rubric weights and voting mode lock at open. Decide them before you press Open nominations.

> **Rule:** Publishing a form or terms change creates a new version. Every nomination keeps the version it was submitted on.

> **Tip:** An edition with steps left shows them as its Overview, so anyone can see what’s missing.
