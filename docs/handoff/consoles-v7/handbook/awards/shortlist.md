# Shortlisting

Drafts are built from approved nominations and the scoring rule. Publishing needs an admin.

Draft shortlist builds a proposal per category. A category below its minimum nominations shows Too few and can’t be published until it reaches it or the minimum is changed in setup.

> **Rule:** Publishing a shortlist needs an admin. Editors and moderators can prepare drafts.

> **Tip:** Publishing sends the finalist letter, which includes how voting works. Check the letter before you publish.
