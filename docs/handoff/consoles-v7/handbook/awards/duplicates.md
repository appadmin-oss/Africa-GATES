# Duplicates and merging

When two nominations are the same person, merge them so votes and evidence land in one place.

1. **Compare side by side.** Name, school, LGA, phone and photo.
2. **Choose the one to keep.** Usually the older one, or the one with consent confirmed.
3. **Merge.** Reasons and evidence from both are kept. Nominators of both are told.

> **Watch out:** A merge can be undone for 24 hours from the audit entry. After that, ask an admin.

> **Rule:** Volunteers can flag a possible duplicate. They can never merge.
