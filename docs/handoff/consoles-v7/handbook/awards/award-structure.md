# Awards, editions and categories

An award runs in editions. Each edition has its own categories, dates, rules and terms. The overall winner of an edition is shown first on results.

Teachers Prize is the award. The 4th Edition is one run of it, with dates from nominations to the ceremony. Categories belong to the edition, so the 4th can add Rural teacher without changing the 3rd.

## The stages

1. **Nominations.** The public nominates. A person approves each nomination.
2. **Shortlist.** Finalists are chosen per category from approved nominations.
3. **Voting.** Free votes and optional contributions. Some editions are judging only.
4. **Judging.** Panels score finalists on the rubric. Judging happens after voting.
5. **Ceremony and results.** Results stay sealed until released, usually at the ceremony cue.

> **Rule:** Judging happens after voting closes, never during it.

> **Rule:** On results, the overall edition winner is shown first, then each category.

An edition can end in one event, or several editions can share one night. The event reads the edition’s dates as locked milestones.
