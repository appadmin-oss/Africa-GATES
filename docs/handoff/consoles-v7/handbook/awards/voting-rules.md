# How voting works

One free vote per verified person per category, plus optional contributions. Never shopping language.

## Free vote

Each person verifies with an email code and gets one free vote per category. Name, phone, email and a message are required; the name shown publicly is optional.

## Contributions

Editions in contribution mode offer tiers (from ₦100). A contribution adds votes and is capped per person per category. The person gets a receipt by email.

| Say | Never say |
|---|---|
| Vote, contribute, support | Buy, purchase, cart, checkout, price |
| Contribution receipt | Order, invoice |
| Votes counted | Votes sold |

## Integrity

- The collusion scan runs every hour during voting. Clusters it finds are held from the tally until a person looks.
- Live totals and the race are public.
- A paid vote that didn’t land is recovered once, from Money › Payments › Vote recovery, and logged.

> **Rule:** Voting is never described in shopping language, anywhere.

> **Rule:** Judging happens after voting closes.
