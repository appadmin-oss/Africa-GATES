# Reviewing nominations

A person approves every nomination. Volunteer checks and platform checks are recommendations; you decide.

Review works oldest first. The desk shows one nomination at a time with the reason, the evidence, the platform’s checks, any volunteer recommendation with their name, and possible duplicates.

## For each nomination

1. **Read the reason against the category.** Does the reason describe what the category rewards? If it fits another category better, say so.
2. **Look at the evidence.** Links open in a new tab. Files are scanned before you see them.
3. **Check the flags.** A volunteer flag is a recommendation, not a decision. Agree or disagree and say why.
4. **Check duplicates.** Same name and school, or same phone, shows as a possible match. Compare before merging.
5. **Decide.** Approve, Return to the nominator with what to fix, or Reject with a reason from the list.

| Decision | What the nominator sees | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Approve | “Your nomination is in.” | It fits the category and the rules. |
| Return | Your note and a link to fix it | Missing evidence, wrong category, fixable. |
| Reject | The reason from the list | Ineligible, abusive, or a duplicate you merged. |

> **Rule:** Every nomination is approved by a person. Nothing is approved automatically.

> **Rule:** The category-fit assistant is shown to admins and judges only. It is never mentioned in public.

> **Watch out:** Never contact a nominee or nominator from your own phone or email. Use the platform so it’s logged.

> **Tip:** Volunteer agreement is sampled: 1 in 5 of their checks is compared with your decision. A note explaining a disagreement helps them improve.
