# Budgets, limits and requests

A lead approves spending up to their limit. Anything above it is a request to finance, with what it does to the budget.

| Amount | Who decides | How |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₦200,000 | The event lead | Approvals › You can approve |
| Over ₦200,000 | Finance | A spend request |
| Over the event’s budget | Finance and an admin | A spend request; the forecast turns red |

## Making a request

1. **Say what and why.** What you need, the amount, when it’s needed, and what happens if it waits.
2. **Check the route line.** It names who decides and how long it usually takes.
3. **Send.** It appears in your Requests and in the decider’s Waiting on you.

## Deciding a request

- Read If approved: it shows the budget effect before you decide.
- Approve, Ask for info, or Decline. Asking or declining needs a note; the person sees it.
- Undo is available for 5 seconds.

> **Rule:** Over-limit spending is never a dead end. The lead can always ask.

> **Rule:** Finance is told at 80% of an event’s budget and again when the forecast passes 100%.
