# Planning an event

An event is a place with its own team, work, money and night. Today tells you what blocks it.

Readiness is the share of work items done, weighted by how much each blocks the night. Today shows the items blocking it, the budget forecast and tickets.

## Work items

Type an item as a sentence: “Book 3 marshals by Fri, Kemi, ₦45,000”. The owner, due date and budget are read from it. Every item has one owner.

1. **Set the date and venue.** Event settings › General.
2. **Link the awards.** Their dates become locked milestones in your timeline.
3. **Add your team.** Event settings › Members. Access ends the day after the event.
4. **Build the plan.** Work, timeline, risks and decisions. Start from a template if one fits.
5. **Set the budget.** Finance sets the budget; you forecast against it.

> **Rule:** Dates that come from an award are locked in the event. They change only in that award’s setup.

> **Tip:** Write decisions down in Plan › Decisions with who and why. Next year’s lead will thank you.
