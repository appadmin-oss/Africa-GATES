# The night: run of show

Cues in order, each with an owner. The results release can be tied to a cue.

Every cue has a time, what happens, who calls it and what’s on the hall screen. Sponsors’ logos run between cues as agreed in their benefits.

1. **Lock the cue sheet 48 hours before.** Changes after that go through the lead.
2. **Rehearse the release cue.** The page, the emails and the screen change together at that cue.
3. **Keep the radio channels on the sheet.** Lead channel 2, safety channel 1.

> **Rule:** Results are released at the cue, not before dinner.
