# After the night

The survey, locked hours and the report, on fixed days.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| The day after | Guest survey goes out. Event access for the team ends. |
| Two days after | Volunteer hours lock from door scans. |
| Within a week | The final report: against budget, against last edition, decisions to keep. |

> **Tip:** Growth compares this edition with the last on the same day before the night, so you can see the trend while there’s still time to act.
