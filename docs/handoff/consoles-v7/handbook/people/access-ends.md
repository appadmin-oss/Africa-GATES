# Access that ends on its own

Every access given for one event or award has an end date. Nobody has to remember to remove it.

- Event leads and teams: the day after the event.
- Moderators on an award: when you set it, often after results.
- Volunteers: each role ends with its award or event.
- Invitations expire after 7 days.

> **Rule:** Expired access removes the place from the person’s menu on their next request.
