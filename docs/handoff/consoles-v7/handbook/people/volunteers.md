# Volunteers

Volunteers work from their own area, never this console. What they check shows here as a recommendation with their name.

## What volunteers can do

| Role | Sees | Never sees |
|---|---|---|
| Nomination check | Reason, category, evidence | Phone numbers, emails, scores |
| Voter drive | Their link’s sign-ups, as counts | Who anyone voted for |
| Door steward | Green or red at their gate | Names on tickets, payments |
| Interview scheduling | Finalists’ slots | Scores, judges’ notes |

## Hours

Most hours log themselves: door scans, accepted checks and verified sign-ups. Volunteers log by hand only for work nobody saw; their lead confirms it.

> **Rule:** Volunteers never see an /admin page or link.

> **Rule:** Data is masked on the server. Hiding it on screen isn’t masking.

> **Tip:** Retention is a second shift. Thank people by name within a day of their first one.
