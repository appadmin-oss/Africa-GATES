# Alert levels

Affects production means people are being failed now. May affect production means they will be if nobody acts.

| Level | Shown as | Act within |
|---|---|---|
| Affects production | Red count on the bell, red pill in the header | Now |
| May affect production | Amber | Today |
| Everything else | Grey | This week |

> **Rule:** Every alert says what to do next and links to the page that fixes it.
