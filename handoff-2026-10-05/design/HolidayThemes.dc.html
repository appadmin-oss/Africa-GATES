Holiday themes

A quiet greeting for signed-in members on My Account, the organisation dashboard and other non-admin dashboards. One banner, a 3px line under the header, and nothing else changes. Public pages, admin and judging never get a theme.

On My Account

Pick a theme to see it on the real page.

The set

Each theme: a tone, a faint pattern on the end side, a glyph, one greeting with the member’s first name, one line, and an optional outlined action.

Phone

Rules

  1. Where: member pages only (/account/*, /org/*, /my-work). Never on public pages, admin, the judges’ console, checkout or a vote.
  2. What changes: one banner at the top of the content and a 3px tone line under the header. Colours, buttons, type and layout stay exactly as they are.
  3. When: from 00:00 to 23:59 in the member’s time zone on the day (the date range for Christmas, New Year and Eid). Eid follows the date the admin confirms; it isn’t computed.
  4. Who: national days (Independence, Democracy, Children’s Day) show only to members whose country matches. Faith holidays show to everyone; a member can switch seasonal greetings off in Settings.
  5. Dismiss: the × hides it for that holiday and year (stored on the account, not a cookie).
  6. Copy: the greeting uses the first name only. The action links into the product (nominate, awards), never to a sale or a donation.
  7. Motion and data: no animation; patterns are CSS, so nothing downloads.
